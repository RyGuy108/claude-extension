How this is measured & privacy

Only a focused Claude tab or this organizer is counted at a time. Composing means editor input in the last 15 seconds; viewing / interaction includes the initial grace period after focus and time since clicks, keys, or scrolling. Locked screens, other apps, background tabs, private windows, and long sampling gaps are excluded. Timing is approximate.

Send attempts detect supported send controls or Enter in the editor; they do not confirm Claude received a prompt. No prompt text, keystroke values, chat titles, or URLs are saved in these statistics. Daily totals stay on this device for 30 days. Pause anytime with the switch above.
