# Privacy

Task Colors for Claude stores task names, colors, title keywords, and preferences in Chrome's local extension storage. It reads Claude tab titles and URLs to display and organize matching tabs, and native group metadata to manage groups. It does not read messages or page bodies, store tab titles or URLs, or send user data to a server.

There are no analytics, third-party services, remote fonts, advertisements, or external code. Uninstalling the extension removes its local preferences. Native groups and open conversations are managed by Chrome and may remain after uninstalling. Chrome and Claude have their own privacy policies and browser/session behavior independent of this extension.
